The emergence of AI is resulting in new developments in terms of operations of businesses, and facility management of corporations is not an exception too. With the help of AI, businesses can ensure better safety, cleanliness, and efficiency of their workplaces.
On the other hand, in case of businesses working with large office spaces, commercial spaces, campus spaces, and corporate facilities, use of technology is not only about keeping up with the pace of time anymore. Technology is becoming an essential part of increasing efficiency and reducing costs.

AI and Its Significance to Facility Management
Facility management uses AI to analyze data and make informed decisions, by collecting data through intelligent means, identifying patterns, automating repetitive processes and making informed decisions.
The technology may help in analyzing facility operations, detecting abnormalities in machine behavior, scheduling cleaning services and making informed conclusions about facility operations. With real-time information on facility operations, facility managers will be able to detect problems before they arise.
Such use of AI is especially beneficial for companies operating in facilities that require coordination between many teams and many services.

AI in Housekeeping Services

Housekeeping services constitute some of the most prominent features of facility management. Indeed, having a neat and clean office is important to ensure that employees are comfortable and hygienic.
Housekeeping services usually involve following set routines and direct supervision by personnel. However, such approach does not always comply with current needs of employees.
Technology-assisted housekeeping will be more responsive. The intelligent system will aid facility managers in monitoring cleaning processes, keeping track of high traffic areas, scheduling staff, and identifying where immediate action is needed.
For example, occupancy information will enable one to know which part of the office building needs more cleaning due to its level of usage. This means that washrooms, cafeterias, meeting rooms, and high traffic areas will all have different cleaning frequencies based on their level of usage.
Professional housekeeping services will now shift from being entirely schedule-based to being data-based.

How Technology Could Help in Improving Corporate Housekeeping Services

Corporate setting requires standards to be adhered to, promptness in implementation, and good supervision. Technology could play an important role in enhancing corporate housekeeping services.
Technology tools such as digital applications may be employed in task allocation, task monitoring, tracking attendance, carrying out inspection, and recording reports. Corporate facility managers can further employ dashboards to find problems and assess performance.

Some technological solutions include:

Housekeeping checklist in digital format
Task tracking system
Workforce monitoring and attendance management
Cleaning inspections
Automated alerts and notifications
Performance reports
Workforce planning with data-driven approach

They decrease the reliance on paper and make it possible for managers to hold accountability at different locations.

Maintenance Prediction: Anticipate Failures before They Become Big Problems
While AI isn’t restricted to house cleaning tasks, maintenance prediction is another area in which AI can revolutionize facility management.
In regular maintenance, there are two kinds of methods employed - scheduled and reactive maintenance. In predictive maintenance, AI is used to detect any irregularities in the working of equipment through sensors.
This will enable organizations to solve their problems much earlier and reduce downtime for critical equipment.
In terms of business premises, this will involve monitoring their air conditioning units, lifts, power supplies, pumps, generators, and other critical infrastructure.

Facilities Decision Making Can Be Improved With AI
The best thing about AI is that it helps to transform large quantities of operational data into valuable information.
Facilities managers can analyze operational data in order to figure out:
What locations need more cleaning resources
Where maintenance problems recur frequently
How effective staff members are at completing assignments
Which assets need regular repair works
What the facilities usage looks like during different hours of the day
What the costs of operations can be reduced
Thus, managers can base their decisions not only on assumptions but also on real data about facilities.

AI Ready: Is Your Facility Equipped for the AI Era?
It doesn’t have to be so complicated to integrate AI. The starting point is to know how your current processes work, then look at where technology can make things better.
Firms can start by making simple digital processes like housekeeping lists, maintenance reports, inspection logs, attendance, and services.
With these processes being established, and data being gathered, companies can eventually start introducing even more advanced technologies like IoT devices, intelligent buildings, predictive analysis, and AI-based monitoring.

This way, the goal will not be technology per se. Each solution will have to lead to improvement in efficiency, safety, quality of service, or financial matters.
The Future of Commercial Housekeeping Services
The future of commercial housekeeping services will be a blend of competent people and intelligent machines. Though AI will be able to handle repetitive tasks and give useful insights, the presence of professionally competent people in facilities is crucial for decision making and quality control among others.
The most efficient model for facility management thus will be an integrated model of people, process, and technology.
There is now less of a need to ask whether AI will impact facility management. The pertinent issue is how well-prepared organizations' facilities are for taking advantage of AI.
Through the implementation of technologies such as housekeeping, maintenance, and monitoring systems, companies can construct facilities that are better prepared for the future.
